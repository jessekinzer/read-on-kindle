Set up your Kindle address

Amazon only delivers email from addresses you approve. Articles are sent from the Google account you sign in with when you first send (in Google Chrome, usually the account Chrome is signed in to), so add that Gmail address to your approved list:

  1. Open Amazon's Manage Your Content and Devices, then Preferences, then Personal Document Settings.
  2. Under Approved Personal Document E-mail List, click Add a new approved e-mail address.
  3. Enter your Gmail address and click Add Address.
